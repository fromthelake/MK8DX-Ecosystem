# Code of conduct

MK8DX Ecosystem exists to make Mario Kart 8 Deluxe more fun for a community of players. The same
spirit applies to this project: be friendly, patient and welcoming, whatever someone's experience,
background or skill level.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you conclude.
- Focus on the problem and the code, not on the person.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's names, accounts, results, recordings or contact details
  without their permission. Use synthetic examples.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what fits the project, and that "not now" is a valid answer.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or imagery, personal or political attacks, unwelcome
sexual attention, threats, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently block a participant. Report a problem privately through GitHub's
**Report content** option or the private vulnerability report described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
(mark it as a conduct report). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
